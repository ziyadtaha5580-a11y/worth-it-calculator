    Straight answers

    A few things
    worth knowing.

    What does cost per use mean?+

    It divides the item's price by how many times you expect to use it over its lifetime. For example, a $240 item used 40 times costs about $6 per use. It is a comparison aid, not a promise about future use.

    How should I read the score?+

    The 0–100 score is an explicitly non-scientific estimate: affordability (40%), cost per use (25%), monthly frequency (15%), and urgency (20%). A result of 75–100 is “Looks Worth It,” 45–74 is “Think It Over,” and 0–44 is “Probably Skip It.” Each component is shown so you can see what moved the number. Affordability scores 100, 75, 50, 25, or 0 as available money covers 100%, 75%, 50%, 25%, or less of the price. Cost-per-use scores are 100 up to 2 currency units, 80 up to 5, 60 up to 10, 35 up to 25, and 10 above that. Monthly use scores are 100 at 12+ uses, 80 at 6+, 60 at 3+, 40 at 1+, and 20 below 1. Urgency scores 100 for “Need it now,” 75 for “Can wait a week,” and 45 for “Just want it.”

    What does “potentially unaffordable” mean?+

    We flag it when projected savings after the purchase and one month of net cash flow would fall below your protected minimum. A monthly shortfall reduces the projection. For the affordability score, available money is savings above the reserve plus one month of net cash flow, but never less than zero. The flag is separate from the score and deserves a closer look.

    Does the calculator give financial advice?+

    No. It makes simple estimates from only the numbers you enter. It does not know your full financial situation, future costs, taxes, or priorities. Treat it as a reflection tool, not financial advice.

    Does my information leave my browser?+

    No. Calculations happen on this device. If you choose to save a result, it is stored in this browser's local storage. Clear your browser storage to remove saved calculations.

    Can I compare prices in different currencies?+

    You can choose CAD, USD, GBP, or EUR for a calculation. No exchange conversion happens, so use the same currency for your purchase and money inputs.
